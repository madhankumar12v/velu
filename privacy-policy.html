Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Velu Memorial Social Trust respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using this website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through this website, including:

  • Your full name
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Email address
  • Your interest, such as volunteering or membership
  • Any message or information you submit through the contact form

We may also receive limited technical information, such as your browser type, device type and general website usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Contact you about volunteering or membership requests
  • Provide information about trust activities
  • Organise and administer community initiatives
  • Improve the website and user experience
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse

3. Contact Forms and Third-Party Services

Our contact form may be processed by Formspree or another service provider used by the Trust. Information submitted through the form may be processed according to that provider’s privacy policy.

This website may also use third-party services such as Google Fonts, Font Awesome, social media platforms and external payment or UPI services. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

This website may use basic browser storage, including local storage, to remember your cookie-notice preference. This information is stored in your browser and is not intended to identify you personally.

You can clear stored browser data through your browser settings.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to your request
  • Operate website services
  • Work with trusted service providers
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal obligations
  • Protect the rights, safety and security of the Trust and others

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage activities, maintain records or comply with legal obligations.

8. External Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms and payment services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

9. Children’s Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

10. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information you have submitted, request correction of inaccurate information or ask us to stop contacting you, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
