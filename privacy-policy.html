Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Velu Memorial Social Trust ("we", "us", "the Trust") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you use our contact or volunteer form, we collect the details you choose to submit, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Any message or information you share with us

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 without a parent or guardian's involvement.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry, volunteering interest, or request
  • To keep you informed about our activities, if you choose to be contacted
  • To improve how we support our community

We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third party.

Form Submissions

Our contact form is processed using a third-party form service (Formspree). When you submit the form, your information is sent securely to our email inbox through that service. Please review Formspree's own privacy policy for how they handle submitted data.

Payments

We do not process or store any payment details on this website. UPI contributions are made directly through your own UPI app to the UPI ID shown on our Support page. We never ask for your UPI PIN or OTP.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics in the future to understand how visitors use the site; this policy will be updated if that changes.

Your Rights

You may ask us to review, correct or delete any personal information you have shared with us, at any time, by contacting us using the details below.
