Who we are

Building a kinder and stronger community

Velu Memorial Social Trust was established on 21 October 2025 with a vision of bringing people together through compassion, unity and positive social impact.

Inspired by enduring values and community spirit, we strive to create opportunities that encourage participation, growth and meaningful contributions to society.

  • Encouraging meaningful community participation
  • Supporting positive social initiatives
  • Creating a lasting legacy of service
