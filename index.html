Quick Mission

Helping citizens apply for Ayushman cards, providing education & digital training, and supporting elderly and needy families.

About Us

Velu Memorial Social Trust is founded by Snega in memory of Late Mr. Velu. Our goal is to continue his legacy of compassion through healthcare, education, and social support initiatives.

Our Services

Ayushman Card Help

Assisting families to check eligibility and complete Ayushman Bharat applications.

Health Camps

Organizing health checkups, blood donation drives, and vaccination awareness programmes.

Social Welfare

Support for elderly, food distribution, and emergency aid in local communities.

Digital Awareness

Helping people use government portals, Aadhaar services, and online forms.

Volunteer Training

Training local volunteers (Velu Sevak) to assist families and spread awareness.

Education Support

Free computer basics, scholarship guidance, and motivation classes for children and youth.

Education Programmes

Digital Literacy Workshops

Learn basic computer skills, smartphone usage, and safe internet practices.

Form-Filling Help

Hands-on assistance with scholarship forms, Ayushman applications, and other government forms.

Student Motivation & Tutoring

After-school sessions for foundational education and exam preparation tips.

Contact & Request Help
