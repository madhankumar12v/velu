Who We Are

Building a Kinder and Stronger Community

Velu Memorial Social Trust began its journey on 21 October 2025 with a vision of contributing to a more compassionate, united and caring society.

Our purpose is to bring people together and create meaningful opportunities to support individuals, families and communities in need.

We believe that positive change does not always begin with something big. It can begin with one person, one helping hand and one meaningful action.

As we grow, we aim to build a responsible and transparent community initiative that encourages participation, volunteering and sustainable social development.

Compassion

Understanding people and responding with care.

Participation

Bringing people together to contribute their time, skills and ideas.

Impact

Turning meaningful actions into positive community outcomes.

In Loving Memory of Velu

Velu was a loving father from Vellore who lived for his wife and three children. Even when his health declined, he chose a government hospital to avoid placing a financial burden on his family. He quietly helped poor and homeless people by sharing food and money. He also saved ₹1,00,000 for his own final rites, so his family would not have to bear the expense. His life of love, sacrifice, compassion and generosity inspired the founding of Velu Memorial Social Trust.

Led by Madhankumar V and a small group of volunteers committed to building this initiative responsibly and transparently.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a compassionate, inclusive and self-reliant society where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity, hope and purpose.

Our Mission

To support individuals and families in need through education, health, basic needs, livelihood development and community initiatives, while encouraging volunteering, participation and meaningful social contribution.

Small actions can create meaningful change when people come together with purpose.
