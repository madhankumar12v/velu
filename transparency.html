Current Status

Our initiative is currently in its foundation stage and is working towards formal Trust registration. Registration details and applicable statutory information will be published here once completed.

No tax deduction benefit is currently being offered or claimed for contributions. Applicable tax approvals will be updated here once obtained.

Current Contribution Method

Founder-managed during the foundation stage. Contributions are currently received on a personal UPI account used for the initiative while formal Trust banking arrangements are being established. This will be updated once a Trust bank account is opened.
